5 questions to ask before hiring a magician for your celebration

To go in with a clear head and avoid last-minute surprises, whatever your celebration.

1

Do they have real experience with the type of celebration I'm planning?

A magician who specialises in kids' birthdays isn't the same as one who specialises in weddings or adult events. Ask directly about their experience with celebrations like yours.

2

Can they show me video of real performances?

A nice website isn't the same as seeing how a real audience reacts during the show. Ask to see footage from an actual celebration before deciding.

3

Does the price include all the necessary equipment?

Always ask before confirming anything, so there are no surprises on the day. Travel, sound and equipment should all be clear from the start.

4

How long is the show and how does it adapt to the space?

A good magician knows how to adjust the length and format depending on the available space, whether it's a garden, a hall or a restaurant, without losing momentum at any point.

5

What happens if something doesn't go as planned?

Questions like "what if the group has very mixed ages?" or "what if the weather turns bad for an outdoor event?" say a lot about a magician's real experience. If the answer sounds improvised, that's a red flag.
