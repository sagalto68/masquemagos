How much does a magician for a christening cost in 2026

September 2026 · 5 min read

If you're planning your child's christening and thinking about hiring a magician for the celebration, the first thing you want to know is the price. Here's an honest guide to how we approach it.

How much does a magician for a christening really cost?

At Más que Magos, our prices for a christening range from €350 to €500. If you're after something more special — a grand illusion show or a baby-appearance effect, for example — we can do that too, with a custom price for that proposal.

A christening happens once. There's no room to "try out" someone inexperienced and see how it goes: it's a day where nothing can go wrong. That's why a good magician, with years of experience working with families and young children, won't charge you less. What you're paying for isn't just the time on stage — it's the guarantee that the moment will go well, with no room for error, in front of the whole family.

What actually determines the price?

Contrary to what many people think, the price doesn't vary much by the length of the performance or the number of guests. It varies mainly by the date and its availability.

Christenings cluster around certain weekends and months of the year, and those dates fill up sooner than most people imagine. When a date is in high demand, availability drops, and that's what makes the price shift from one date to another.

Our recommendation: book early. The sooner you lock in the date, the easier it is to secure the best price and the availability you need, instead of ending up with fewer options — and worse terms — at the last minute.

What's included in the price

The quote includes the full performance, adapted to your christening's format, with all the necessary professional material. If you want something beyond a standard performance — like a baby-appearance effect or a grand illusion moment designed specifically for the occasion — we'll talk it through and give you a specific quote for that proposal.

Why it's worth not choosing on price alone

It's tempting to compare quotes and go with the cheapest one, but at a christening that can cost you in a different way: it's a one-time opportunity, with the whole family gathered, and there's no second take. A truly experienced magician knows how to read the room, adapt to children of different ages, and hold the attention of adults and kids at once. That's not something you can improvise — and it's exactly what you're paying for.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the price vary by date and not by duration?

Because higher-demand dates have less availability. Booking in advance is the best way to secure the best price and the date you need.

Does magic work as well with young children as with adults at a christening?

Yes. A christening usually has both children and adults, so the show is adapted to include an interactive moment for the little ones and close-up magic that surprises the adults too.
