How Much Does a Magician for a Communion Cost in 2026

Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

If you're planning your child's communion, you've probably wondered how much it costs to hire a magician and what a fair price looks like. Here's an honest guide with real market prices in Spain.

The average price of a magician for a communion

In Spain, a professional magician for a communion typically costs between €300 and €450 for a one-hour show. This is the range where most magicians with real experience in family events sit, and the one we recommend for this type of celebration.

There are offers under €200, which usually come from magicians still learning the craft, and high-profile magicians who charge over €1,000. For a communion with guaranteed quality, the fair price sits in that middle range.

What factors make the price vary

1. Date and time

Communions cluster between April and June, so Saturdays and Sundays in those months are the most in-demand and book up first. Booking ahead gives you more room on price and availability.

2. Show length

A 1-hour show is standard, usually plenty to keep both kids and adults entertained. Longer shows naturally cost more.

3. Travel

If the magician has to travel outside their usual area, it's normal for a travel fee to apply. Many magicians, us included, don't charge for travel within a reasonable distance.

4. The magician's experience and specialization

A magician who specializes purely in kids' entertainment isn't the same as one with real communion experience, where you need to connect with children and adults at the same time. That ability to adapt the show to every age is what separates a decent show from a memorable one.

How to know if the price is fair

Rather than just looking at the number, check these before hiring:

  • Do they have real reviews from other communions, not just a nice website?
  • Can you see a video of an actual performance?
  • Does the price include all the material, or are there hidden extras?
  • Do they reply quickly and clearly when you ask questions?

A good communion magician isn't necessarily the most expensive, but is almost never the cheapest. The sweet spot is usually that €300-450 range where you find serious professionals with real experience.
