HOW IT WORKS

What information is needed to request a quote?

With the date, the type of event, the number of attendees and the available space, a proposal can already be prepared. The more details provided, the better it can be tailored.

What type of corporate events can you cover?

Team building, incentive trips, conferences and conventions, trade fairs, dinners and Christmas events, product launches and any corporate celebration. If your event doesn't fit any category, just get in touch anyway.

What type of show works best for our event?

It depends on the event's goal. To break the ice or encourage networking, close-up magic among attendees usually works best. For a closing moment or a central part of the event, a stage show for the whole group has more impact.

What does the service include?

It can range from a full turnkey service that includes everything —equipment, sound system, set-up and take-down— to hiring just the magician, if the company or event agency prefers to handle the rest.

Do you work across all of Spain or only in Madrid and Barcelona?

We work across all of Spain. We travel wherever the event is, whether in a major city or any other location.

Can you invoice companies and do you work with event agencies?

Yes. We invoice companies directly and regularly work with event and communication agencies, integrating into the production they already have in place.
