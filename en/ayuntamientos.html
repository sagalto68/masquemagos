HOW IT WORKS

What information is needed to request a quote?

With the date, the type of celebration, the available space and the expected audience, a proposal can already be prepared. The more details provided, the better it can be tailored.

What type of show works best for us?

It doesn't need to be decided in advance. Depending on the type of event —local festival, medieval fair, parade, activity for the elderly...— the format of show that works best for each space and audience is recommended.

What does the service include?

It can range from a full turnkey service that includes everything —equipment, sound system, set-up and take-down— to hiring just the magician, if the town council prefers to handle the rest.

Is there also season programming for municipal theatres?

Yes. As well as one-off festivals, there is also availability for season programming in municipal theatres, with shows designed to fill the venue throughout the year. With different magicians and different shows available, distinct performances can be scheduled at different points in the season, keeping magic on the programme all year round — something audiences tend to love, and which helps fill the theatre every time.

How much does a magician cost for a town festival?

The price depends on the format, the duration and the needs of the town. Each proposal is tailored to the budget and what the town council requests.
