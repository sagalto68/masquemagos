Magic for the corporate Christmas dinner: ideas so it's not just another dinner

September 2026 · 3 min read

The Christmas dinner is the corporate event that comes round most often, and the hardest one to make different. These ideas will give this year's dinner a moment of its own without complicating the planning.

The problem with the Christmas dinner: everyone knows what's going to happen

The same kind of restaurant, a set menu, a few words from management and a toast. There's nothing wrong with that, but it makes one year blur into the next. The difference between just another dinner and the one people are still talking about in the office on Monday is usually a single moment nobody saw coming.

Magic fits that role well because it doesn't force you to change anything in the plan: it adapts to the venue, the timings and the number of people you already have.

Five ideas to add magic to the dinner

1. Close-up magic at the welcome drinks

While people arrive and wait standing up, the magician moves from group to group. It's the best moment to mix departments that barely cross paths during the year. We explain how it works in detail in this article on corporate dinners.

2. A Secret Santa with a twist

If you're doing Secret Santa, the magician can turn the gift exchange into a performance. For example, revealing who gave what to whom before anyone opens their present, or making a gift appear where nobody expects it.

3. The prediction of the year

As dinner begins, the magician hands a sealed envelope to someone from management, who keeps it all night. It's opened at the toast, and inside is written something that was decided during the dinner. A perfect way to close the year.

4. A touch of company branding

Companies such as Disney, Honda and SEAT have used magic to launch products. At a Christmas dinner, the same idea can make the logo, the name of the year's big project or a message for the team appear in the middle of a trick.

5. A short finale before the toast

If you'd rather have a moment everyone shares, a 15 to 20-minute act just before the speeches brings the room together without making the night longer.

When to book: Christmas dinners are concentrated in the last weeks of November and in December, and the most popular dates fill up first. If you already have a venue and a date, ideally book the magic in October or early November too.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should we book a magician for the corporate Christmas dinner?

Ideally in October or early November. December dates are the first to fill up.

Does magic work in a restaurant without a stage?

Yes. Close-up magic needs no stage, lighting or sound: it happens at the tables or among the groups.
